- `gwi log` stops when its output pipe closes: the backlog scan ends at the first failed write, so a one-shot `gwi log | head -1` no longer parses the rest of the file and `gwi log -f | head -1` exits instead of polling on. `--follow` notices a closed pipe on its next write, so an idle follow lingers until the next matching record. `gwi log --follow` also re-reads the log from the top when `gwi log prune` or rotation replaces it with a file as large as the old offset, which it detects by device and inode on unix (#32).
